How to Play

🟢 GREEN — Same Order
Say: dog → cat → lion
🔴 RED — Reverse Order
Say: lion → cat → dog

Press Play to watch the circles appear one by one first. Then the demonstration selects them in the correct order. They stay visible until the selection begins. When the sequence is complete, the microphone turns on. Say each emoji name one at a time, or tap the circles. Press Restart to restart your timed game from the beginning.

Tap an item to hear its English name. These words match your selected category and difficulty.
